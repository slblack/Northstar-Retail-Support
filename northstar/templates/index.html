About this demo

  • This is a fictional retail-support demonstration using synthetic data. It is not connected to any real order or customer system.
  • Sending a message transmits it, the system instructions and retrieved store-policy excerpts to OpenAI, and (on the protected path) to Cisco AI Defense. A fully allowed run makes two model generations and three inspection calls; provider costs, quotas and data policies apply.
  • Answers on the two paths are generated independently from identical inputs and can differ. The path without AI Defense still has the model’s own safety behaviour.
  • Suggested-question labels are evaluation annotations only. Live AI Defense responses alone decide what is blocked.
